# 🌲 Vanrakshak: Forest Fire Early Detection System

## 📝 Simple Summary
**Vanrakshak** (meaning "Forest Protector") is a smart monitoring system designed to prevent wildfires in high-risk forest belts across India. It uses "virtual sensors" to monitor temperature, smoke levels, and humidity in real-time.

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Real-Time Simulation**: Simulates hundreds of sensors across 50+ regions (like Kangra, Shimla, and Wayanad).
- **Early Warning Map**: A live dashboard with a map that shows "Safe," "Warning," and "Critical" zones.
- **Smart Risk Scoring**: Automatically detects if a fire is likely based on combined sensor data.
- **Online Database**: Stores all your logs and alerts securely in the cloud (Aiven MySQL).

## 👥 Who is it for?
1. **Department Heads**: To monitor the whole state and manage critical alerts.
2. **Field Employees**: To receive instant notifications about risks in their specific zones.
